## 2. What the bot does

The bot counts Index rolls and claims in the event channel, shows leaderboards and stats, and
runs an event with vouchers, keys, chests, quests, bounties, daily rewards, trading and profile
cards. See `/guide` or `xeguide` for how it works.

  through a bug, cheating or a mistake.
- Chest odds, prices, stock and rewards can change at any time.
- Event items may be reset, changed or removed, for example between events or after a bug.

- put offensive, hateful or harmful text in your profile bio.

Staff can take away items, reset your progress, remove your bio or block you from the event
if you break these rules.
